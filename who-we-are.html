Who we are

AI built for psychiatry billing — by people who know the revenue cycle

LucidClaim exists to make clinical coding faster, more accurate, and defensible — so billing teams spend less time on manual rework and more time on analysis that protects revenue.

Our mission

Leverage AI to transform the revenue cycle

We use AI to significantly increase the efficiency and effectiveness of revenue cycle operations — re-purposing billing staff effort from manual coding tasks to analytical work that catches denials, captures HCC revenue, and keeps claims audit-ready.

◎

Psychiatry-first

Built around psychiatry clinical notes, CPT psychotherapy add-ons, MDM rules, and the denial patterns that hit mental health practices hardest.

◎

Human-in-the-loop

AI proposes codes and flags risk; credentialed coders review every verdict. Technology accelerates experts — it does not replace them.

◎

Compliance by design

HIPAA and SOC2 compliant Snowflake environment. Full audit trail on every AI output, human override, and reprocess event.

◎

Practical AI

MVP live on Snowflake Cortex and Claude Opus 4.5 — deployed where healthcare data already lives, not in a disconnected black box.
