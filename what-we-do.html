Built-In Denial Prevention

Catches issues before they reach the payer

1

MDM Lowest Element Rule

AMA 2021 framework — supportable E/M level capped at the lowest of 3 MDM elements.

2

Prescription Specificity Check

Flags "medication managed" entries missing drug name, dose, action, and rationale.

3

Psychotherapy Add-On Validation

Time-band verification (90833 / 90836 / 90838) plus 7 required content elements.

4

Administrative Integrity

Signature timing, POS consistency, HPI adequacy for 99214 / 99215.

5

14 Integrity Flag Types

UPCODING_RISK, MDM_LEVEL_MISMATCH, ADMIN_DEFICIENCY, HPI_INADEQUATE, and more.
